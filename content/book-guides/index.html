How the shelf was made

Books converted from PDF with pymupdf4llm; each guide's content extracted by a reading agent under a checked schema (every quote verified verbatim against the book, every chapter heading real, 25–35 cards per book with at least six per level); pages generated by scripts/build_book_guides.py; acceptance gates in GATES-book-guides.md.
