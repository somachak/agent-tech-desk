Tutorial §9.9–9.10. yield turns a function into a generator. Callers iterate values over time instead of receiving one big list.

yield produces a value and pauses. Generators are lazy iterators.

Cosmetic AI Assistant streams the reply into the chat pane. Word chunks are a simple stand-in for token streaming — same consumption pattern.

Change the splitter to yield pairs of words (hint: iterate indices).

A for loop is the everyday consumer. next() and list() show the same protocol underneath.

for x in gen: … is the idiomatic consumer; next(gen) pulls one value.
